CORE THESIS:

South Africa does not have a shortage of capable people.

It has a discovery, trust and proof-of-experience problem.

Millions of people possess useful practical skills and already perform informal, temporary and once-off work — including cleaning, gardening, tutoring, hair services, repairs, painting, catering, photography, moving assistance, technology support, automotive work and other services.

Yet much of this economic activity remains fragmented across word-of-mouth referrals, WhatsApp groups, Facebook/community groups and personal networks.

For the worker, this causes two problems.

First, finding the next paying opportunity is difficult.

Second, informal work frequently disappears once the job is finished.

A worker may have painted 40 homes, repaired hundreds of devices, maintained gardens for years or worked repeatedly for local businesses but still have no formal CV, LinkedIn profile, qualification or portable digital evidence showing that experience.

Their capability exists.

Their professional record often does not.

At the same time, households, entrepreneurs and small businesses regularly need affordable short-term help but struggle to answer:

Who nearby can do this?
Are they available?
Can I trust them?
Have they done this before?
Were previous customers satisfied?

SideGigs connects these two sides.

It is a hyperlocal marketplace for short-term and informal work where people can discover opportunities and where customers can discover capable people nearby.

But SideGigs is more than a gig marketplace.

EVERY COMPLETED GIG BECOMES CAREER CAPITAL.

After legitimate work is completed, SideGigs can add that experience to the worker's digital portfolio:

completed gigs
skills demonstrated
ratings
customer reviews
references
work evidence/photos
reliability history
experience

This creates a portable digital work identity for people whose experience has traditionally gone undocumented.

Someone without a conventional CV can eventually say:

"Do not only take my word for it. Here is the work I have actually done."

SideGigs therefore creates:

INCOME TODAY.
EVIDENCE OF EMPLOYABILITY TOMORROW.

This feature is central.

SideGigs is helping digitise economically active people who may currently be professionally invisible.

A completed SideGigs job should create a verified work-history item.

The portfolio should communicate:

WHAT THEY CAN DO
WHAT THEY HAVE DONE
WHAT PEOPLE SAY ABOUT THEM
HOW RELIABLY THEY COMPLETE WORK

This creates trust for customers and upward mobility for workers.

Over time a worker should be able to use SideGigs history to pursue better opportunities — inside or outside SideGigs.

BUSINESS MODEL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SideGigs uses a transaction-based marketplace model.

Workers join free.

The worker should receive the amount advertised/agreed.

SideGigs adds a service/protection fee to the customer.

Hackathon MVP default:

15%.

Example:

Gig payout:
R500

SideGigs fee:
R75

Customer pays:
R575

Worker earns:
R500

SideGigs earns:
R75

The fee supports marketplace operations and platform protection mechanisms.

Future models may include:

business subscriptions
promoted opportunities
premium verification
enterprise hiring/talent discovery
recruitment partnerships

These are future opportunities, not required MVP features.
