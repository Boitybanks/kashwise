The concept is an independent hackathon prototype; no FNB endorsement or connected banking service is claimed.

## Why this emphasises the moat

The memorable benefit is the worker's accumulating evidence and credibility, grounded in client-confirmed jobs.
The spoken pitch uses customer language; [PROOFSCRIPT.md](PROOFSCRIPT.md) states the defensibility hypothesis and
its limits for judges. Portable records remain part of the product.
