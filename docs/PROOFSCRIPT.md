# ProofScript — your work transcript

**Your skills. Your work. Your proof.**

ProofScript is the product name for KashWise's existing worker profile and portfolio of evidence, formerly called
Work Passport. It brings a CV-style skills/profile summary together with records of completed, client-confirmed work,
supporting work photos and client reviews. A new name does not create a new verification process.

## How the record grows

1. The worker completes a job through the existing job lifecycle.
2. The client's confirmation creates the portfolio record. Workers cannot type themselves a confirmed job.
3. Work photos and the client's review add context to that record. User-supplied photos do not independently prove a
   skill, an identity or a payment.
4. Confirmed records support the existing skills profile and downloadable, digitally signed work history.
5. A separately posted payment-provider settlement upgrades the evidence to payment-confirmed. Completion is not
   proof of settlement or payout, and payments remain simulated in this prototype.

Existing links (`/w/:id`), QR codes, signed-document types and verification contracts keep their identities. The
worker can share their profile and download signed work history. Private earnings remain private.

## The intended moat

The defensibility hypothesis is **evidence that accumulates through real work**: a record with client provenance,
work samples, repeated customer relationships and increasing context about demonstrated skills. A directory entry
can be copied; a deep, trustworthy local history requires completed work and participation from customers.

This can improve the usefulness of a worker's profile for future clients and make returning to KashWise worthwhile.
It is a hypothesis to validate in a pilot, not a claim that a proven moat or bank acceptance already exists. Workers
retain share/download options. Retention should come from usefulness and continued opportunities.

## Naming and copy

- Product: **KashWise ProofScript**.
- Worker screen: **My ProofScript**.
- Descriptor: **Your work transcript**.
- Supporting line: **Your skills. Your work. Your proof.**
- Pitch: **Every client-confirmed job grows your ProofScript. More work. More proof.**

"Client-confirmed" and "payment confirmed" stay distinct. Do not describe ProofScript as a government credential,
credit score, bank-approved CV, guarantee of employment or accepted bank proof of income. Existing simulation labels
and signature checks continue to apply.
